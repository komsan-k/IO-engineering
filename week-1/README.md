# Week 1 : Chapter 1 - Foundations & Hardware Interfacing

Chapter 1 introduces the fundamentals of **microcontroller programming and hardware interfacing**, progressing from low-level digital control to communication with external peripherals. Students develop practical skills in **GPIO, interrupts, timers, ADC, PWM, I²C, and SPI** through hands-on laboratory activities.

## Learning Flow

**GPIO & Registers → Interrupts & Timers → ADC & PWM → I²C & SPI**

## Topics

| Slot | Topic | Key Skills |
|------|-------|------------|
| **1** | GPIO, Registers & Bare-Metal Control | MCU architecture, registers, GPIO, LED/button interfacing |
| **2** | Hardware Interrupts & Timers | ISR, polling, debouncing, hardware timers |
| **3** | Analog Interfacing & PWM | ADC, sensor acquisition, PWM, actuator control |
| **4** | Serial Protocols | I²C, SPI, register access, UART output |

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of Chapter 1, students should be able to:

- Configure basic MCU peripherals.
- Acquire digital and analog inputs.
- Control outputs and actuators.
- Respond to hardware events using interrupts and timers.
- Communicate with external devices using standard serial protocols.
